Plan your bills

Choose whether to pay a bill on its due date or reserve money from one or two earlier paychecks. A $500 car payment can be allocated as $250 from each of two paychecks. Due dates remain visible for reference.

The app selects the last one or two payments from this income source on or before each bill due date. This is a planned allocation, not an automatic payment.

Build your savings

Savings contributions are planned on every income date, not actual transfers. If essential expenses exceed income, prioritize necessities.

Payday advances & loan repayments

Enter the amount actually owed and the next repayment date. Existing obligations are shown with upcoming bills. Reductions on the Exit Plan only change hypothetical later-cycle repayments; they do not erase current debts.

Lender-by-lender exit plan

Amounts come from Payday Advances. Choose how much to reduce future new borrowing after each repayment. Existing debts remain due in full. Hypothetical repeating repayments on later dates reflect your selected reductions.

This is a scenario tool, not a promise of lender extensions or future advances. Reducing new borrowing requires enough cash to cover the gap. If your cash flow is negative, use Get Help.

Get help

Ask lenders about available repayment arrangements before a scheduled withdrawal. You can also seek assistance through 211, NFCC credit counseling, or the CFPB complaint portal.
